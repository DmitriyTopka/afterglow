# Eval v6 (07.10.2026): the agent after the judge-style fixes

## What changed since v5
A smoke test of 30 requests written blind, the way a first-time judge would type them (`eval/judge30.json`), showed three failures:
10 of 30 got a question and no picks, a named format was ignored ("like Breaking Bad but it's a book" gave no book), and age was ignored
(The Hunger Games for a 6-year-old, Deadpool 2 for a 14-year-old). Fixes in `lib/agent/agentic.ts`:

- A question alone is no longer an answer. If the message says anything (an occasion, an age, a topic), the agent is sent back to turn it
  into two or three well-known works and recommend. When it still asks (for "hi"), the question comes with the store's most popular titles.
- Formats named in the message are guaranteed in the candidates and in the five picks; "on vinyl" or "but it's a book" means only that format.
- Every catalog title now has a minimum age from its official rating (`age_min`, `scripts/age_ratings.mts`); titles above the recipient's age are dropped.

## Judge-style smoke test, before and after
`eval/judge30_runs_before.json`, `eval/judge30_runs_after.json` (and a re-run of the weak cases).
- Question with no picks: 10 of 30 before, 0 after (two still ask, with popular picks shown alongside).
- Format: "90s hip hop on vinyl" now returns only vinyl; "like Breaking Bad but a book" returns five novels.
- Age: the 6-year-old gets The Lion King, Frozen, Coco, Minecraft and Stardew Valley; the 14-year-old no longer gets an R-rated film.
- Still weak: requests the catalog barely covers (true crime, cooking, 90s East Coast hip hop specifically).

## The 40 frozen blind scenarios (same as v2-v5)
Run `npx tsx scripts/eval_v5.mts eval/v6_runs.json`, score `python3 scripts/score_v5.py eval/v6_runs.json`.
New top-3 picks no judge had seen (18) went to a blind judge: 4 yes, 14 no (`eval/pool_v6_verdicts.json`).

| | labels P@3 | pooled P@3 | wins / losses vs Claude (labels) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Agent v6, even 20 (never tuned on) | 0.50 | 0.65 | 10 / 4 |
| Agent v5, even 20 | 0.52 | 0.63 | 11 / 4 |
| Claude alone, even 20 | 0.37 | 0.55 | |
| Agent v6, all 40 | 0.45 | 0.68 | 20 / 6 |

The fixes did not move the ranking quality on the frozen set (one top-3 hit in 60 either way on labels). 10 wins to 4 is a lead on a
small sample, not proof (two-sided sign test p ≈ 0.18).
